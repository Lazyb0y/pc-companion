# Support

- **Questions and ideas:** ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/Lazyb0y/pc-companion/discussions).
- **Bugs:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/Lazyb0y/pc-companion/issues/new/choose). Include the version from **General > About**, your Windows version, and the steps that cause the problem. Logs help: **General > Logs > Open logs folder**. For a problem that's hard to explain, turn on **General > Detailed logging**, make the problem happen again, then attach the logs. Look through them before you attach them.
- **Feature requests:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/Lazyb0y/pc-companion/issues/new/choose).
- **Security problems:** report them privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

PC Companion is in early development. Answers depend on the maintainer's time, and there's no guaranteed response time.
