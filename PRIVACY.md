# Privacy

- PC Companion doesn't collect, send or share any data about you. It has no telemetry, analytics, ads or accounts, and uploads nothing.
- **Update check:** once a day, and each time you select **Check now** on the **General** page, PC Companion asks GitHub for its latest release, at `api.github.com/repos/Lazyb0y/pc-companion/releases/latest`. The request is the same for everyone and contains nothing about you or your PC; as with any website, GitHub sees your IP address, under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). When a newer version exists, PC Companion tells you, and you download and install it yourself. To stop the daily check, turn off **Check for updates** on the **General** page; **Check now** asks only when you select it. PC Companion makes no other network connections.
- It stores these files on your PC, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PCCompanion`:
  - `settings.json`: your settings.
  - `update-check.json`: when PC Companion last checked for updates and the newest version it found.
  - `Logs`: diagnostic logs, kept for 14 days. **Detailed logging**, if you turn it on, adds more detail for 24 hours. Your user profile folder in them is replaced with `%USERPROFILE%`, and they contain no passwords or file contents. They leave your PC only if you share them yourself, for example by attaching them to an issue.
- The installer adds a startup entry so the app can start when you sign in; it stays in the background only if you turn on **Start with Windows**.
- Uninstalling removes the app, its startup entry, and your settings and logs in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PCCompanion`. To keep your settings, run the installer again, choose **Remove** and check **Keep my settings**, or uninstall with `KEEPSETTINGS=1`, for example `msiexec /x PCCompanion-x64.msi KEEPSETTINGS=1`. If others use PC Companion on the same PC, their settings and logs stay until they delete their own `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PCCompanion`.
- **Clipboard Actions:** reads the clipboard's text only when you press its shortcut, and never text that the app that copied it marked private, as password managers do. The text stays in memory until the action you choose starts; PC Companion doesn't store, log or send it. A link or a rule's web address opens in your browser, which sends the copied text to that site.
- **Network Profiles:** changes your proxy settings for Ethernet and Wi-Fi only when you choose a profile, in the PC Companion icon's menu or on the feature's page, and reads them, and the Group Policy settings that manage them, to show which profile is in use. Your profiles are in `settings.json`; their names, proxy servers and addresses aren't logged. PC Companion makes no network connections for it: with automatic detection or a setup script, Windows and your apps look for the proxy and download the script, as with Windows' own proxy settings.
- **Website:** the project website, [lazyb0y.github.io/pc-companion](https://lazyb0y.github.io/pc-companion/), is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no analytics or cookies and loads nothing from other sites. If you choose a theme with its switch, your browser keeps the choice in its local storage for the site; it's never sent anywhere. GitHub logs the IP address of each visitor, for security, under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
